What CustodyTap is

CustodyTap records a chain of custody for removable media, laptops, test equipment and similar items, using signed NFC tags and tap-to-confirm actions on a phone. It produces custody histories, use logs, exception reports and destruction certificates, and lets you export your records. It is a recordkeeping tool that supports your compliance evidence; it does not itself make you compliant with any law or standard, and we do not provide legal, regulatory or audit advice.

The tamper-evident record

CustodyTap chains events by hash and emails each day's chain head to your administrators so that a change or deletion is detectable. This is a strong safeguard, not a guarantee against every possible failure or attack. You should keep the daily chain-head emails and your exports, which are what let you prove integrity independently of us.
